GIVE SANTA A REAL VOICE

Record the Mayday (phone voice memo is fine), save it as mayday.mp3 in this folder, and push.
The site plays it through a radio filter, so it sounds like it's coming over the handset.

SCRIPT (deep, puffed, a bit panicked; about 15 seconds)

  "Elf HQ, Elf HQ, this is Sleigh One."
  "Bringing her down for a rest stop in the park."
  "Location is..."            (2 seconds of silence)
  "Weather's turning fast. Wind's picking up. Hail..."
  "Losing control..."         (1 second of silence)
  "Words are..."              (stop mid-sentence)

Tips: leave the gaps silent; the site adds static on top. Rustle a jacket or blow near the mic for wind.
